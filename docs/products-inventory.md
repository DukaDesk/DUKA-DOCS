# Products & Inventory

Routes: `/dashboard/products`, `/dashboard/inventory`

**Products** is your catalog (what customers buy). **Inventory** is your stock (how much you have).

## Products

1. Go to **Products** → **Add product**.
2. Fill: name, price, old price (for discounts), description, badge (e.g. `Popular`, `Low`), photo, category.
3. Organize with categories — these become the filter pills in your customer app's Inventory screen.

## Inventory

1. Go to **Inventory** to see stock levels per item.
2. Watch **Stock alerts** (e.g. `Chapman — 3 left, reorder point 10`).
3. Restock before you hit zero — low-stock items automatically surface on the Dashboard.

## Good habits

- Use clear photos and short descriptions — they render in the menu grid of your mobile app.
- Set a **reorder point** per item so alerts fire early.
- Archive out-of-season items instead of deleting them (keeps order history clean).
