# Analytics & Reports

Route: `/dashboard/analytics`

Analytics answers: *are we growing, what sells, who comes back?*

## Key metrics

- **Revenue** and % change vs last month.
- **Orders**, **average basket**, **repeat-customer rate**.
- **Top sellers** with units and ratings.
- **Monthly goal** progress bar.

## Weekly routine

1. Compare revenue and orders week over week.
2. Open the top seller — feature it on Home and promos.
3. Check repeat rate. Below ~30%? Send a win-back promo from Marketing.
4. Export the report before month-end for your books.
