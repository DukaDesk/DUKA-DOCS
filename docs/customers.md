# Customers

Route: `/dashboard/customers`

Customers lists everyone who has ordered, booked, or messaged you.

## What to do here

- **Look up a customer** — orders, spend, messages, loyalty.
- **Message them** — jump straight into Messages with context.
- **Fix profiles** — merge duplicates, correct phone numbers.

Customer data powers the **Profile & Settings** screen in your mobile app (name, email, notification preferences).
