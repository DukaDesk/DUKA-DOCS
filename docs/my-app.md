# My App (What Is Live)

Route: `/my-app`

My App shows the **currently published customer app**: screens, tabs, branding, and version.

## Use it to

- Confirm the latest **Publish** actually went live.
- Walk every bottom tab as a customer would.
- Spot missing links or empty screens before announcing the release.

If something looks stale, go back to the builder and **Publish** again, then re-check here.
