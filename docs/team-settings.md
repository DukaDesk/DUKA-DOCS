# Team, Profile & Settings

Routes: `/dashboard/team`, `/dashboard/profile`, `/dashboard/settings`

## Team

- Invite staff by email, assign roles (`tenant_owner`, `business_manager`, `store_manager`, `sales_staff`, `content_manager`…).
- Roles gate sidebar pages automatically — e.g. sales staff only sees Dashboard, Orders, Customers, Messages.
- Remove leavers promptly.

## Profile

- Your personal details and password. Keep the recovery email current.

## Settings

- **App Data**: logo, app name, category, slug (`my-app` format — lowercase, numbers, hyphens).
- **Brand color**: flows into hero banners, buttons and the customer app theme.
- Changing the **slug** changes public links — do it rarely.
