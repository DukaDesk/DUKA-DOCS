# Billing & Subscription

Route: `/dashboard/billing`

Manage your **DukaDesk plan, payment methods and invoices**.

1. See the **current plan** (price, renewal date, seats).
2. Track **usage** for the month.
3. Update the **payment method** (e.g. Visa •• 4521).
4. Download **invoices** for accounting.

Failed charges? Update the card, then retry — expired cards also block Publish-related paid features.
