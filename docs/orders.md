# Orders

Route: `/dashboard/orders`

Use Orders to **confirm, prepare, fulfill and refund** customer purchases, and to download receipts.

## Order lifecycle

1. **Pending** — new order, confirm it.
2. **Preparing** — kitchen / packing stage.
3. **Delivered / Completed** — handed over or fulfilled.
4. Refunds go back to wallet or bank within 48 hours (see your policy in Help content).

## How to work an order

1. Open **Orders** and search by order number or item.
2. Click the order to see items, customer, total, payment and fulfillment status.
3. Advance its status as you work. The customer sees the update in the mobile app.
4. For receipts, use **Download receipts**.

## Tips

- Keep the **fulfillment status accurate** — it drives the Transaction / Order History screen in your customer app.
- Handle failed payments from **Billing** records and message the customer from **Messages**.
