# Getting Started

## 1. Create your account

1. Open the Merchant app and go to **Sign Up** (`/signup`).
2. Enter your **name, email and password**. Confirm the OTP if asked.
3. You land on **Onboarding** (`/onboarding`) if your shop has no category yet.

## 2. Complete onboarding

1. Enter your **business name** — this becomes your default app name.
2. Pick a **category** (food, retail, services, etc.). This controls which modules appear in your sidebar.
3. Set your **brand color** and upload a **logo**. These flow into your customer app automatically.
4. Save. You are redirected to **Overview** (`/dashboard`).

## 3. Verify compliance (KYC)

Some features — especially **Desk Design / Publish** — require compliance.

1. Go to **Compliance** (`/compliance`).
2. Upload the requested business documents.
3. Wait for approval. The sidebar unlocks **Desk Design** once approved.

## 4. Add your first product

1. Go to **Products** (`/dashboard/products`).
2. Click **Add product**. Fill name, price, description, photo and category.
3. Check it appears in **Inventory** (`/dashboard/inventory`) with correct stock.

## 5. Invite your team (optional)

1. Go to **Team** (`/dashboard/team`).
2. Invite by email and assign a role: `business_manager`, `store_manager`, `sales_staff`, etc.
3. Each role only sees the pages it needs — e.g. sales staff sees Dashboard, Orders, Customers, Messages.

## 6. What to do next

- Run the shop daily from [Dashboard](./dashboard.md) and [Orders](./orders.md).
- Build your customer app from [App Builder overview](./app-builder/overview.md).
- Publish when ready: [Publish](./app-builder/publish.md), then verify in [My App](./my-app.md).
